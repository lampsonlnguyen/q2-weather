## Tomorrow.io weather icons

Most weather icons in this directory were adapted from the
[Tomorrow.io Weather Code Icons](https://github.com/Tomorrow-IO-API/tomorrow-weather-codes),
which are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

**Powered by Tomorrow.io** — [tomorrow.io/weather-api](https://www.tomorrow.io/weather-api/)

The images were renamed to Q2 Weather's condition IDs and resized to 24 × 24
pixels at compile time. Those resize operations are modifications. The weather
forecast data itself comes from Open-Meteo, not Tomorrow.io.
